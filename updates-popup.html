WHAT'S NEW

Gamelist updates

Shelf is now part of Gamelist:

  • Your Shelf collection has now been integrated with Gamelist!

    Open Shelf next to Backlog, Radar (previously Upcoming), and Wishlist (previously Available). Manage physical and digital games, owners, regions, conditions, and prices right next to your Backlog and wanted games.

  • Add to Backlog also sends the game to Shelf

    When you move a game from Radar or Wishlist to Backlog, you can add its physical or digital details to Shelf in the same step. Use Add Game to choose Collection, Radar, or Wishlist, and turn on Add to backlog to add a Collection game to both lists.

  • Showcase is now a component in Gamelist

    Add Showcase to your page layout and choose up to five games from Shelf to feature.
