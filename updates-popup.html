WHAT'S NEW

New platform accounts updates

Here’s what changed with the account setups for achievement and time tracking:

  • Playtime fills in automatically

    When you mark a game Completed or Finished, Gamelist now can get its playtime from your linked Steam, PlayStation, Xbox or Nintendo accounts. You can change the number yourself or use the reload button to fetch it again.

  • The big N is here. Connect your Nintendo account from Settings!

    Choose Log in with Nintendo, select your account, then copy the link address from Select this person and paste it into Gamelist.

  • A new Platform account connection system!

    You can now connect IGDB, PlayStation, Xbox, Steam, and Nintendo directly from Settings. No more secrets needed! Follow the instructions shown for each platform. They are still as secure, and you probably don't need to fill them in again using the new system, except PlayStation. Feel free to delete the PSN secret and setup from here.
